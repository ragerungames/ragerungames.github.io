How it works

Record your game. Cut. Post.

Built for posting a Short of your game every day, without fighting a big video editor.

Cut on a real timeline

Video, text, voice, sound and music rows. Drag clips in, trim, split, reorder, undo. Space plays, S splits, T adds text, and the mouse wheel zooms. Rest the mouse on a clip in the library to peek at it.

Text that reads on a phone

18 text looks (hits, calm captions, pills and the house title), 17 fonts included or your own, and your own colours, outlines, shadows and boxes. Every text pops, slams, types or counts in, and can wobble or pulse while it's on screen.

Design your end card

Pick a moving background, put in your logo or your game's name, type a line like "Out now", choose the colours and how it comes in. One click makes the video, and every Short ends with your call to action.

Export every version, then post

One click renders each version you set up (for example "Out now" and "Wishlist") in the background. You get the title, description, captions and tags for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels, X and Reddit, ready to copy.

Questions

Does it record my screen?

No, it edits clips you've already recorded. Record with OBS, your engine's recorder or Windows Game Bar, then edit here. Using Unity? Our Shorts Maker for Unity records the Game view inside Unity.

Does it upload my videos?

No. Everything stays on your PC: no account, no tracking. The posting texts are ready to copy, and "Copy & open" opens the upload page so you can drag the file in yourself.

"Windows protected your PC"?

The app is new and not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn you the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway. You only do this once. Unsure? Email us before you run it.

Can I use the videos commercially?

Yes. The videos you make are yours. Use clips, music and fonts you have the rights to; the fonts and sounds that come with the app are free to use in your videos.
