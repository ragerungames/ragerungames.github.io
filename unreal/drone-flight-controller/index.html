Drone Flight Controller Pro

Quick Start · Fly in five minutes · Unreal Engine 5.8

1. Welcome

Drone Flight Controller Pro flies quad, hexa and octo drones with real thrust and torque, momentum, wind, ground cushion and prop wash, yet stays friendly for beginners. Everything is plain C++ with Blueprint access, and every setting has a tooltip.

In the box

  • Three flight modes (Cinematic, Sport, Acro) plus an Arcade preset, six handling presets, battery and wind.
  • Drone AI: hover, follow, orbit, chase, wander, waypoints, racing, follow-me filming, patrol with vision cones, formations, return home to charge.
  • Winch, cargo, depots and a delivery game; combat with turrets, four weapons, lock-on and fighting AI.
  • Cinematic tools: six automatic camera shots, flight recorder and replay, camera paths and shot sequences.
  • Drone Creator: design your own drone (12 starters, 8 palettes, camo, patterns, dirt and wear) and save it as a ready-to-fly Blueprint.
  • Flight HUD, four drone cameras with a gimbal, generated motor and impact sounds, prop blur, downwash dust, sparks, smoke and speed trails.
  • Setup Window with Check & Fix, 5 model drones, 11 designed drones, 6 demo maps.

4. The three flight modes

Cinematic (1)
The sticks set speed; let go and it brakes and holds position. Smooth and slow, like a camera drone.
Sport (2)
The sticks set the tilt; it levels itself and holds its height. Fast.
Acro (3)
The sticks set rotation rates; it keeps any angle. Flips and rolls, like an FPV racer.

Real-world effects

  • Prop wash: dropping straight down through your own air makes the drone wobble; move sideways while descending.
  • Ground cushion: near the ground the drone floats on its own air.
  • Wind: gusts push the drone around (check the windsock).
  • Crashes: hit something hard and the motors stop; press R to respawn.

5. Add a drone to your level

  1. Open the Setup Window (Tools > Drone Flight Controller > Setup Window).
  2. Overview: click a drone to place it where the viewport looks, or drag it into the viewport.
  3. Drone: pick a preset (Beginner, Camera Drone, FPV Racer, Heavy Lifter, Tiny Whoop, Arcade).
  4. Check & Fix: click Fix All, then press Play.

Your own model

Import the model with Combine Meshes off so each propeller is its own mesh. Select the meshes in the Content Browser and choose Create Drone Blueprint (right-click) or Make Selected Model a Drone (Tools menu). Propellers are found by name (prop, rotor, blade, fan) or by shape, and get motors and spin directions.

6. Make your own drone

  1. Open Tools > Drone Flight Controller > Drone Creator.
  2. Start: pick one of 12 starters or roll a Random Drone (tick parts to keep).
  3. Frame, Body, Rotors, Parts: shape it; quick size buttons scale the whole drone from a 75 mm whoop to a 1.8 m heavy lifter.
  4. Paint: palettes, camo styles (Woodland, Desert, Urban, Arctic, Night), patterns, dirt and wear.
  5. Flight: how it flies and how heavy it is.
  6. Save: you get DA_<Name> (the design) and BP_Drone_<Name> (a ready-to-fly drone). Double-click a design to edit it again.

7. Add combat in two clicks

  1. Pick your drone in the Setup Window, open Combat, click Player Fighter.
  2. Pick another drone, click Enemy or Practice Target. Press Play.

The crosshair follows the mouse and the turret follows the crosshair. Hold the crosshair on an enemy until LOCKED for homing missiles. Shots allow for your own speed, so strafing still hits.

8. Deliver, film and patrol

  • Deliver: add a Winch (Setup Window > Delivery), lower the hook with Z onto cargo, raise with X, let go with V. Depots and the Delivery Game turn it into timed jobs.
  • Film: B plays cinematic shots, C onboard gives the gimbal, K / L record and replay, Shot Sequences play whole edits with titles and fades.
  • AI: AI Drones page > quick setups (Follow-me Cam, Patrol Guard, Formation Wing, Racer, Orbit Cam, Wanderer). O in game watches them.
