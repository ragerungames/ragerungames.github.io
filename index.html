About the studio

Made by a game developer, for game developers.

RageRun Games is an independent studio focused on game development and asset creation. We love making games, and we package the systems we build so other developers can ship theirs faster.

Our speciality is movement. Riding, flying, hovering, skating and balancing are the parts of a game players feel in every second, and the parts that take longest to get right. Each controller starts from a playable prototype and is tuned until it is fun before it ever reaches the store.

The newest line leans into silly physics: googly eyes, wobbly bodies, ragdoll falls and climb-backs, local multiplayer and CPU opponents, so a controller can become a party game in an afternoon.
