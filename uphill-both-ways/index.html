How it plays

If you don't talk, you don't move

If you do talk, you probably still crash.

Lean together

Stand on top of a rolling snowball with a friend. Lean forward to go faster, step back to brake, shift your weight to steer.

Roll it down

Six worlds, one mountain, from a snowy summit to a sunny beach. Jump crevasses, dodge icicles and outrun an avalanche.

Grow it. Lose it.

The snowball is your health and your battering ram. Roll through powder and it grows. Hit a rock and it loses chunks.

Bowl the crowd

Penguins line the course to cheer you on. Some wave. Some dive away. Some freeze, flippers up, and accept their fate.

Don't melt

Reach the hot springs and the beach too small and the snowball melts away under you.

Now ride it back up

The Climb takes the snowball back up the mountain. Lean too little and it rolls back. One mistake can undo ten minutes of work. Uphill. Both ways.
