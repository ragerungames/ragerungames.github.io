Assembling the ropes
0%
Backspace back to menu Movement - W / A / S / D – Pump, steer and walk - E / Q – Climb - Shift – Run Rope Controls - Space – Let go / Hop - Hold Space – Prevent catching a rope - Tap Space in Air – Grab a rope / Kick off a wall Tricks - Ctrl + W + S – Front flip + Backflip - Ctrl + A + D – Spin (in air or on rope) - Ctrl – Tuck Grappling - Hold LMB – Left grapple line - Hold RMB – Right grapple line - Hold LMB + RMB – Dual grapple lines Additional Controls - Tab – Slow motion - R – Respawn

Pro Tip: Let go while rising at approximately 40° for a perfect launch.
