Click the game first so it gets your keys. Press T mid-drive to fold the wheels into fans and take off; settle onto the ground to drive again. In the air after a jump: WS flip, AD spin, QE roll. Race the AI cars round the canyon, fly through the sky rings, or try the loop, mega ramp and half-pipe. A gamepad works too.
