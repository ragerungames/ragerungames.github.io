Privacy Policy: RageRun Comedy Sound Synth and ragerungames.com

Effective 23 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data is handled when you use RageRun Comedy Sound Synth (the "Software"), try its free web demo, visit ragerungames.com (the "Site") or contact us. The controller is Muhammad Khizar, trading as RageRun Games, Karachi, Pakistan. Contact: support@ragerungames.com.

1. The Software: we collect nothing

  • The Software runs on your own computer. The app opens in its own window and talks only to a small local server on your computer (127.0.0.1), which isn't reachable from the internet.
  • Your sounds, projects (.csynth files), settings, favourites and exports stay on your computer. We don't receive them.
  • Video Sync: a video you pick in the Gag Builder is played from your own disk inside the app. It is never uploaded or copied anywhere.
  • Comedy-fy and imported sounds: sounds you bring in are copied into the app's data folder on your computer (%LOCALAPPDATA%\RageRun Comedy Sound Synth\). They don't leave it.
  • The Software has no account, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting. It doesn't send us any data.
  • The Software has no update check. Links in the app (website, Discord, email) open in your browser or mail app only when you click them. Apart from those, it makes no internet connections.

2. The free web demo

The web demo runs in your browser. Your sounds are made on your device and never sent to us. It keeps your demo settings in your browser's local storage (on your device only) and the Save button downloads the file to your computer. Clearing your browser data removes the settings.

3. Buying

Purchases are handled by itch.io. itch.io collects your payment and email address and delivers the download. itch.io's privacy policy applies. We receive the purchase details that itch.io shares with sellers (such as your email address if you choose to share it, and the order amount and country). We use them only to provide the product, support and refunds, and to keep tax and accounting records.

4. The Site

  • The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to run and secure the service (see GitHub's privacy statement).
  • Pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends a request, including your IP address, to Google when a page opens.
  • The product page's buy button loads a script from itch.io, which may set cookies or receive your IP address when you open the purchase pop-up.
  • Visit counters: some pages count visits and button clicks with a simple counter service (abacus.jasoncameron.dev). Your browser sends it a request when the page loads or a button is clicked, so it receives your IP address and browser details like any website. We only see the totals. The Site uses no advertising trackers and sets no cookies of its own.

5. Support

If you email us or message us on Discord, we use your name, email address or Discord username and your message to help you. Discord's own privacy policy applies to Discord. We keep support emails for up to 24 months unless they're needed longer, for example for a refund or tax record.

6. Legal bases (for visitors in the EU/UK)

Contract (to deliver what you bought and support it), legitimate interests (to answer messages and keep the Site secure) and legal obligation (tax records).

7. Sharing

We don't sell personal data and don't share it except with the services named above, or when the law requires us to.

8. International transfers

We're based in Pakistan, and itch.io, GitHub, Google and Discord operate in other countries, so data may be processed outside your country.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its use. Email us and we'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

10. Children

The Software and Site aren't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes

We'll post any changes here with a new effective date.
