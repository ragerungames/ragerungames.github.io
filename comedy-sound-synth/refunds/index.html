Refund Policy: RageRun Comedy Sound Synth

Effective 23 November 2026

We want you to be happy with RageRun Comedy Sound Synth. If it doesn't work for you, we'll make it right.

14-day refunds

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of purchase if:

  • the Software won't install or run on your PC, and we couldn't fix it together;
  • a feature described on the product page doesn't work, and we can't fix it quickly;
  • you bought it twice by mistake.

After 14 days we normally don't give refunds, but write to us anyway if something is wrong.

Try the free web demo at https://ragerungames.com/demos/comedy-sound-synth/ first if you're not sure the sounds are what you want. It has 4 of the 42 sound types.

How to ask

1. Email support@ragerungames.com with the email address you used to buy and your itch.io order or download link.

2. Tell us briefly what went wrong. If it's a technical problem, we'll try to fix it first. Most problems are solved in one or two messages.

3. If a refund is due, we ask itch.io to refund it to your original payment method. Payment providers usually take 5-10 business days to return the money.

How refunds work

Purchases are processed by itch.io, which collects the payment and hosts the download. Refunds go back the same way. Please don't open a chargeback with your bank before contacting us: a chargeback takes longer and costs both of us fees.

After a refund, your licence ends and you must delete the Software. Sounds you already made and used in your projects while the licence was valid can stay in those projects (see section 12 of the EULA).
